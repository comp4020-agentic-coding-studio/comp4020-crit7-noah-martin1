# Reflections

### 1. What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

Rather than randomly selecting an ANU system based on it being the oldest or, verifiably, the worst, I chose to reflect on a time or process that I had personally struggled with and identify the system connected to that experience. As I approach the end of my degree, I have been using ANU Hub, a relatively new system ANU introduced to manage your degree. However, in my experience, it is far from acceptable by today's standards. This personal experience motivated me to improve the system for this crit.

### 2. What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

I think meaningful change, and personal connection to that change, should be emphasised in software development. Developers can easily get caught up in the tech race, building systems and creating solutions to problems that do not necessarily exist. I think grounding yourself and your work in something meaningful is important. It gives the work a clear purpose and makes its impact more tangible and measurable, because the boundaries of the problem have been understood and addressed first.
